# Halloran & Sons (trade-service) — asset provenance

Halloran & Sons is a fictional second-generation heating and plumbing firm built
as a **Concept preview**. It is not a real business. The firm, the family (Dermot,
Kieran, Sean, Bernadette), the engineers, the town of Ashcombe and every place,
street and travel time in it, the customers and their reviews, the commercial
clients, and every figure on the site are invented and illustrative.

There are **no currency amounts anywhere** in this concept. Rates are expressed
the way a tradesman says them out loud — no callout fee, a fixed first-hour rate
then by the half hour, a written quote before anything long, parts at cost plus a
flat handling charge, one flat out-of-hours surcharge.

## Credentials, deliberately generic

No accreditation body, trade association, certification scheme, insurer, or
registration number is named or invented anywhere on this concept. Where the copy
touches qualifications it stays generic and unverifiable on purpose ("every
engineer on our vans is gas-qualified and carries their card", "we hold public
liability cover"), and the footer states outright that these are illustrative
rather than a real accreditation.

## Phone numbers and email

Both numbers — `01632 960 118` and `01632 960 204` — sit inside the `01632 96xxxx`
range reserved for fictional use in drama and documentation. The prefix is not
allocated to any real geographic area, so the `tel:` affordances in the header and
footer cannot reach a real person. Email uses the reserved `.example` top-level
domain (`hello@halloran.example`).

## Runtime assets

`assets: []` in `src/lib/templates/trade-service.ts` — this concept ships and
hotlinks **no raster runtime assets at all**.

- Every image surface is **token-derived**. The demo twins render their
  backend-free `bg-muted` placeholders, and
  `src/components/site/templates/trade-service/theme.css` repaints them as
  corrugated steel under a worklamp: a cool highlight from above, 13px vertical
  ribs mixed from `--hs-ink`, a safety-orange pool low right, and shade falling
  across the diagonal. The one nested plate (`content-image-frame` on the Areas
  page) becomes hazard chevrons — `repeating-linear-gradient` in `--hs-orange`
  and `--hs-steel` — framing that panel, so it reads as a works van with its
  doors open. The `feature-cards-media` still life is repainted as a parts tray
  from the same tokens.
- The home hero's letterbox still, its scroll-velocity ticker diamonds, and the
  plate's raking sweep are generated by the `hero-kinetic` block itself from
  `--brand` / `--background` / `--foreground` via `color-mix`. This template only
  retunes those tokens.
- Review "avatars" are the shared twins' monogram placeholders, restyled here as
  squared ID-card tiles. **No likeness, real or synthetic, is depicted anywhere.**
- The client marks in the logo strip come from the shared demo twin
  (`src/components/site/demos/DemoLogos.tsx`) — invented monochrome lockups for
  fictional companies, carrying no third-party trademark. On this concept they
  read as commercial customers, not as certifying bodies.
- The Halloran mark is the firm's initial on a solid safety-orange square, set in
  the fonts the root layout already loads — plain CSS in
  `src/components/site/templates/trade-service/lockup.tsx`, no SVG or image file.
- Typography uses only the fonts the root layout already loads via `next/font`
  (Geist Sans and Geist Mono). This concept ships no font files, and it is the one
  template that uses **no** Instrument Serif: the `hero-kinetic` closing word's
  serif italic is neutralised back to upright heavy sans in `theme.css`, because a
  refined serif accent reads wrong on a plumber's signboard. The one graphic move
  in its place is a painted safety-orange `text-decoration` rule under that word.
- Colour is entirely scoped CSS variables under
  `[data-template-theme='trade-service']`. The site itself stays forced-light;
  nothing here touches `:root`, `.dark`, or `globals.css`. Safety orange is never
  used as a text colour — it is a field, a rule, or a glow, with `--hs-rust` and
  `--hs-amber` carrying any orange that has to be read.

The `stats-proof` twin renders a static customer wordmark ("NORTHWIND") that
belongs to the shared demo twin, not to this concept; the theme restyles it as a
small tracked lockup and the quote beside it is attributed to that fictional
lettings firm.

## Generated posters

`public/templates/trade-service/posters/*.jpg` are **build artifacts** —
deterministic screenshots produced by `tools/templates/capture.ts`
(`pnpm templates:capture`) from this repository's own preview routes at the
template's current `revision`. They are screenshots of this repository's rendered
output and carry the repository license. Regenerate them whenever the concept's
visual direction changes, and bump `revision` in the same change.
